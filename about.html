About Bright Little Learners

Bright Little Learners is an independent educational website for children, parents, caregivers and teachers who enjoy discovering how the world works.

We publish accessible explanations, family-friendly science activities, nature observations and questions that encourage curiosity. Our aim is to make learning feel practical, welcoming and fun—not to replace school, professional advice or adult supervision.

How we create content

Our guides are written to explain basic ideas in clear language and suggest simple ways to explore them. Activities should be adapted to a child's age and abilities, and a responsible adult should supervise hands-on experiments.

Our promise

We aim to keep our content useful, honest and respectful of readers. We do not promise that every experiment will work exactly the same way in every setting.
